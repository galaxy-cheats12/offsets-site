1. What we collect

We do not collect personal information directly on this website. The Service is a static site that does not run analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or cookies. The information we receive depends on how you interact with the Service:

We use the information described above to:

3. Payment processors

Payment is handled entirely by third-party processors. The processor's own privacy policy governs how they handle your payment data. When you complete a purchase, you are providing your payment information directly to that processor, not to us. The processor's privacy policy is available on their website.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The third-party payment processor's checkout page may set cookies as part of its operation; those cookies are governed by the processor's privacy policy, not this one.

5. Data retention

We retain purchase records (email address, product, amount, transaction ID) for as long as required by applicable tax and accounting law, typically seven years. We retain support emails for as long as needed to resolve the issue and for a reasonable period afterward for reference.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties except:
